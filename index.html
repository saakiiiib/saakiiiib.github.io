01 / ABOUT

Professional ProfileSUMMARY · EDUCATION · RESEARCH INTERESTS · OBJECTIVE

Simulation engineer focused on photovoltaic device physics and applied machine learning — from semiconductor device modeling to thermal management of power systems.

My work centers on building accurate numerical models, validating them against published data, and using them to guide engineering decisions. I have modeled perovskite and thin-film solar cells in SCAPS-1D, analyzed heat transfer in power-electronics hardware with COMSOL Multiphysics, and developed ML models that predict device performance from material parameters.

I am equally comfortable producing professional electrical documentation with AutoCAD Electrical and writing clean Python for data analysis, automation, and visualization. Every result I publish is reproducible, documented, and reviewed — this portfolio is the curated summary of that work.
