About

Simulation with honest validation

I am an undergraduate researcher in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Islamic University, Kushtia, Bangladesh, working with Prof. Dr. Md. Abdullah-Al Mashud.

My research looks at tin-based perovskite solar cells, a lead-free alternative to today's lead perovskites. I compare absorbers in one fixed device structure, split the recombination losses to see what limits each device, and train machine-learning surrogates whose test sets contain only absorber configurations the model never saw.

Outside research I build tools that automate simulation and design work in Python, COMSOL Multiphysics and AutoCAD Electrical.

Research

Current and past work

Lead-free CsSnI₃, CsSnBr₃ and CsSnCl₃ solar cells in one device structure

Ongoing · undergraduate thesis research · manuscript in preparation
  • Simulated the three tin perovskites in a single Al/ITO/WS₂/CsSnX₃/NiOₓ/Au structure in SCAPS-1D, with every input parameter taken from published studies, and optimized each absorber.
  • Split the recombination current into bulk, radiative, Auger, interface and contact channels, and used separate front and back interface tests to find which loss limits each device.
  • Built a 9,800-device simulation dataset and trained five machine-learning models with grouped cross-validation, so near-identical devices never sit on both sides of a test. SHAP rankings are checked against the device physics.
