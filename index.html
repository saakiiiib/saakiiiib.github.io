01 · About

Physics first, then the model.

I'm an undergraduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Islamic University, Kushtia, Bangladesh. My interest sits where semiconductor physics, optoelectronics and materials science meet: how a material's band structure and defects decide what a solar cell can do.

Right now I study tin-based perovskites, a lead-free route for thin-film photovoltaics. I compare absorbers inside one fixed device, split the recombination current to see what limits each one, and test machine-learning surrogates only on absorber designs they have never seen.

Outside research, I write tools that automate simulation and design work in Python, COMSOL Multiphysics and AutoCAD Electrical.

How the device works

Light in, carriers out.

A photon is absorbed in the perovskite and makes an electron and a hole. The band offsets push electrons toward WS₂ and holes toward NiOₓ. Every defect and interface along the way is a chance to lose them, and that loss is what my work measures.
